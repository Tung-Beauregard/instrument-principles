XRF X 射線螢光光譜儀 能量認出元素

X 射線管的 X 射線照進樣品,把原子內層的電子打掉;外層電子掉下來補位,多出的能量變成一道新的 X 射線(螢光),能量只看是哪一種元素。偵測器一道一道量能量,畫成能譜,每種元素是一組峰。不必溶解樣品,但只看得到表面一層。選一個樣品按「測量」,再在能譜上移動游標看看。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 在能譜上移動游標或點一下,讀出那個能量、對到的譜線與計數;「對數」讓很小的峰也看得到
  • 「測量」:偵測器一道一道數 X 射線,約 6 秒跑完設定的測量時間,能譜慢慢長高;換電壓、濾片或時間再量一次,上一次會留著比較
  • 管電壓:X 射線最高的能量就是電壓(50 kV 最高 50 keV)。能量要超過吸收邊限才打得掉那一層的電子:砷的 K 層要 11.87 keV,所以 10 kV 量不到砷;銠靶自己的 K 線要 23.2 kV 以上才出現
  • 濾片(0.1 mm 鋁片):擋掉低能量的 X 射線,8 keV 以下的背景幾乎消失,鉀、鈣、鉻這些低能量的峰也變弱,砷只少一成多
  • 含量 = 淨計數 ÷ 靈敏度,靈敏度要用同材質的標準品(或理論計算)先求出來;± 只算計數的誤差,時間多 4 倍誤差才減半
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Move the cursor over the spectrum or click it to read the energy, the matching line and the counts; "Log" makes very small peaks visible
  • "Measure": the detector counts the X-rays one by one, runs through the set measuring time in about 6 seconds, and the spectrum slowly grows; measure again with another voltage, filter or time and the previous spectrum stays for comparison
  • Tube voltage: the highest X-ray energy equals the voltage (at most 50 keV at 50 kV). Only X-rays above the absorption edge can knock out an electron from that shell: the arsenic K shell needs 11.87 keV, so at 10 kV there is no arsenic; the rhodium K lines of the tube itself appear only above 23.2 kV
  • Filter (0.1 mm aluminum): it blocks low-energy X-rays, so the background below 8 keV almost disappears and low-energy peaks such as potassium, calcium and chromium get weaker, while arsenic drops only by a little over a tenth
  • Amount = net counts ÷ sensitivity; the sensitivity comes first from standards of the same material (or from calculation); the ± counts only the counting error, and 4 times the time halves it
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。以常見的桌上型能量分散式 XRF 為例:銠靶 X 射線管(4 到 50 kV、幾十瓦、氣冷)、可換的濾片與準直器、樣品放在上方的量測窗、從下方照射,矽漂移偵測器(SDD,用帕耳帖致冷片降溫,不用液態氮;解析度約 135 eV,以錳 Kα 計)。元件的位置、大小與散射角(約 100°)是示意。真的儀器有安全連鎖,X 射線開著時面板上的 X-RAYS ON 燈會亮,機殼外的劑量率在 1 µSv/h 以下;「展開」只是為了看裡面。能譜是計算的示意:譜線能量取自 NIST 的資料庫;峰高用簡化的基本參數法估算,X 射線管的連續 X 射線與銠的特性 X 射線各算一半;背景、散射峰的大小與計數多寡是示意;木材裡鉀、鈣、錳、鐵的小峰、空氣的氬峰、銠 L 線與土壤的銣、鍶小峰也是示意。CCA 處理材的含量由保留量 6.4 kg/m³ 換算(假設木材密度 500 kg/m³),銅唑處理材用 3.3 kg/m³,土壤是 NIST SRM 2710a 的認證值;含量的換算是示意,不能拿來鑑定樣品。另一種 XRF(波長分散式)先用晶體依波長把 X 射線分開再數,解析度比較好。

Schematic. Modeled on a common benchtop energy-dispersive XRF: a rhodium-target X-ray tube (4 to 50 kV, tens of watts, air-cooled), exchangeable filters and collimators, the sample on a window on top, lit from below, and a silicon drift detector (SDD, cooled by a Peltier element instead of liquid nitrogen; resolution about 135 eV at manganese Kα). Part positions, sizes and the scattering angle (about 100°) are illustrative. A real instrument has safety interlocks, an X-RAYS ON lamp lights while X-rays are on, and the dose rate outside the case is 1 µSv/h or less; "Open" is only for looking inside. The spectra are calculated illustrations: line energies come from the NIST database; peak heights are estimated with a simplified fundamental-parameter model in which the tube's continuous X-rays and the rhodium lines each count for half; the background, scatter peak sizes and count levels are illustrative, and so are the small potassium, calcium, manganese and iron peaks of wood, the argon peak from air, the rhodium L line and the small rubidium and strontium peaks of the soil. Amounts in CCA wood are converted from a retention of 6.4 kg/m³ (assuming a wood density of 500 kg/m³), copper azole wood uses 3.3 kg/m³, and the soil uses the certified values of NIST SRM 2710a; the conversion to amounts is illustrative and must not be used to identify real samples. The other kind of XRF (wavelength-dispersive) first separates the X-rays by wavelength with a crystal and then counts them, giving better resolution.
