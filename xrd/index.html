XRD X 射線繞射儀 角度對了才亮

X 射線的波長和原子之間的距離差不多(約 0.15 nm)。照到原子排得整整齊齊的晶體,一層層原子反射的 X 射線只在某些角度互相加強(布拉格定律)。X 射線管和偵測器一起轉,記下每個角度的強度,就是繞射圖:尖峰表示排得整齊(結晶),寬寬的隆起表示沒排整齊(非晶)。拖曳偵測器或按「掃描」看看。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 拖曳偵測器或 X 射線管上下轉,或用繞射角滑桿,改變 2θ;兩邊一起轉,各和水平夾 θ,樣品不動(只在原地慢慢自轉)
  • 「掃描」:管子與偵測器從低角度轉到高角度,記下每個 2θ 的計數,約 6 秒畫完一張繞射圖;換鎳濾片或晶粒大小再掃一次,上一張會留著比較
  • 鎳濾片:銅靶除了 Kα 還有比較弱的 Kβ;鎳濾片吸掉大部分的 Kβ,Kα 也只剩約四成。拿掉它,每個強峰左邊會多一個 Kβ 的小峰
  • 晶粒大小:原子排得整整齊齊的一小塊(微晶)有多大,不是粉末顆粒的大小。越小峰越寬,謝樂公式 D = Kλ ÷ (β cosθ)
  • 結晶度指數(Segal)= (主峰高度 − 18° 的高度) ÷ 主峰高度。只是一種估法:沒有扣背景、只看一個峰,換方法算出來差很多
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Drag the detector or the X-ray tube up or down, or use the angle slider, to change 2θ; both arms turn together, each at θ above level, while the sample stays put (it only spins slowly in place)
  • "Scan": the tube and detector turn from low to high angles and record the counts at each 2θ, drawing a full pattern in about 6 seconds; scan again with another filter or crystal size and the previous pattern stays for comparison
  • Nickel filter: besides Kα, the copper target also gives a weaker Kβ line; the nickel filter absorbs most of the Kβ, and lets through only about 40% of the Kα. Take it out and a small Kβ peak appears left of each strong peak
  • Crystal size: how big the neatly ordered blocks of atoms (crystallites) are, not the size of the powder grains. The smaller they are, the broader the peaks; Scherrer equation D = Kλ ÷ (β cosθ)
  • Crystallinity index (Segal) = (main peak height − height at 18°) ÷ main peak height. It is only one estimate: no background is subtracted and only one peak is used, so other methods give very different numbers
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。以常見的粉末 X 射線繞射儀為例(Bragg-Brentano 光路、θ–θ 測角儀、銅靶、閃爍計數器),測角儀半徑在模型裡約 20 cm;元件位置、狹縫與光束的寬度都放大或簡化過。X 射線看不見,這裡用淡紫色畫。真的儀器關在有門鎖的輻射防護櫃裡,開門 X 射線就會關掉;「展開」只是為了看裡面。繞射圖是模擬的:峰的位置由布拉格定律與晶格常數算出(銅 Kα1 0.15405929 nm、Kα2 0.15444274 nm、Kβ 0.1392234 nm);食鹽與矽的相對峰高取自 1953 年美國國家標準局的標準圖譜,纖維素的相對強度是由晶體結構計算的值;峰寬、背景、雜訊、非晶隆起的寬度與晶粒大小的選項都是示意,不是實測數據。

Schematic. Modeled on a common powder X-ray diffractometer (Bragg-Brentano beam path, θ–θ goniometer, copper target, scintillation counter); the goniometer radius in the model is about 20 cm, and part positions, slit and beam widths are enlarged or simplified. X-rays are invisible and are drawn pale violet here. A real instrument sits inside a radiation enclosure with door locks, and opening the door switches the X-rays off; "Open" is only for looking inside. The patterns are simulated: peak positions come from Bragg's law and the lattice constants (Cu Kα1 0.15405929 nm, Kα2 0.15444274 nm, Kβ 0.1392234 nm); relative peak heights for salt and silicon come from the 1953 US National Bureau of Standards reference patterns, and relative intensities for cellulose are values calculated from its crystal structure; peak widths, background, noise, the width of the amorphous hump and the crystal size options are illustrative, not measured data.
