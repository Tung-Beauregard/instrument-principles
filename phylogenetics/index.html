演化樹 從序列推親緣關係

把同源的序列排好,算出兩兩之間的距離,再用 UPGMA、鄰接法、簡約法或概似法推出樹;bootstrap 告訴你每個分支在重抽之下穩不穩。最後用萜類合成酶家族看:序列最像的基因,產物不一定一樣。

自己調整

章節

    重點整理

    先看:DNA 定序、萜類合成酶

    Read first: DNA sequencing, Terpene synthases

    • 多序列比對的前提是每一欄都來自同一個祖先位置;有空格或對不準的欄,建樹前通常整欄拿掉。
    • p 距離 = 不同的欄 ÷ 比較的欄。同一個位置換過不只一次時 p 會低估,Jukes–Cantor 校正 d = −(3/4)·ln(1 − 4p/3),d 的單位是每個位置的替換數;p 接近 0.75 時 d 趨近無限大(飽和)。
    • UPGMA 把最近的兩群合併、節點高度取距離的一半,等於假設各支演化一樣快;速率差很多時會把演化快的序列放錯。鄰接法用 Q(i,j) = (n − 2)·d(i,j) − r(i) − r(j) 找鄰居,距離剛好吻合某棵樹時一定還原那棵樹,不需要等速。
    • 簡約法找改變次數最少的樹,概似法找在替換模型下最可能產生這組序列的樹;n 條序列的無根樹有 (2n − 5)!! 種(10 條就有 2,027,025 種),只能用搜尋法找。
    • 讀樹:分支(clade)是一個節點和它所有的後代;繞節點轉動不改變關係,樹梢的上下順序沒有意義;根要靠外群,中點定根要假設等速。
    • Bootstrap 把欄位抽出放回重抽、重建樹,支持度是分支出現的比例。Felsenstein(1985)以 95% 為顯著;Hillis 與 Bull(1993)在等速、對稱的樹上模擬,70% 以上多半對應 95% 以上的正確機率。方法本身會錯時,錯的分支一樣拿到高支持。
    • 植物萜類合成酶分成 TPS-a 到 TPS-h 共 7 群,裸子植物的 TPS-d 再分 d1、d2、d3。序列能看出屬於哪一群,產物要做出酵素、用 GC-MS 確認(做法見萜類合成酶與基因功能)。
    • A multiple sequence alignment assumes that every column comes from the same ancestral position; columns with gaps or poor alignment are usually removed whole before building a tree.
    • p-distance = differing columns ÷ compared columns. When a position has changed more than once, p underestimates; the Jukes–Cantor correction is d = −(3/4)·ln(1 − 4p/3), with d in substitutions per site, and as p approaches 0.75, d goes to infinity (saturation).
    • UPGMA merges the two closest groups and places each node at half their distance, which assumes that every branch evolves at the same rate; when rates differ a lot, it misplaces fast-evolving sequences. Neighbor joining finds neighbors with Q(i,j) = (n − 2)·d(i,j) − r(i) − r(j); when the distances fit a tree exactly, it always recovers that tree, and it does not need equal rates.
    • Parsimony looks for the tree with the fewest changes; likelihood looks for the tree most likely to produce these sequences under a substitution model. n sequences have (2n − 5)!! unrooted trees (2,027,025 for 10 sequences), so the best tree can only be found by searching.
    • Reading a tree: a clade is a node and all of its descendants; rotating around a node does not change the relationships, and the top-to-bottom order of the tips means nothing; the root comes from an outgroup, and midpoint rooting assumes equal rates.
    • The bootstrap resamples columns with replacement and rebuilds the tree; the support is the share of trees in which a clade appears. Felsenstein (1985) took 95% as significant; Hillis and Bull (1993), simulating equal-rate, symmetric trees, found that 70% or more usually corresponds to at least a 95% chance of being correct. When the method itself is wrong, wrong clades get high support too.
    • Plant terpene synthases fall into 7 groups, TPS-a to TPS-h, and the gymnosperm TPS-d splits further into d1, d2 and d3. The sequence shows which group a gene belongs to; the product has to be confirmed by making the enzyme and running GC-MS (see Terpene synthases and Gene function).
