拉曼光譜儀 散射光換了顏色

雷射照在樣品上,散射回來的光幾乎都和雷射同色(瑞立散射);散射光裡大約一百萬到一千萬個才有一個少了一點能量、波長變長(拉曼散射),少掉的正好是化學鍵振動的能量。濾掉雷射原色,用光柵依波長散開、CCD 一次記下,就是拉曼光譜。拖曳 CCD 或點譜圖選位置,或按「測量」。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 拖曳 CCD 晶片上的亮線,或在譜圖上點一下、拖曳,移動游標;左上的讀值卡寫出那一欄的拉曼位移與真正的波長,能階圖跟著改
  • 「橫軸」:拉曼位移是比雷射少了多少能量(cm⁻¹,每公分幾個波),波長是光真正的波長(nm);換雷射時看哪一個不變
  • 「測量」:CCD 曝光(畫面快轉),再由左往右讀出整條光譜;換雷射、曝光時間再量一次,上一次會留著比較
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Drag the bright line on the CCD chip, or click and drag on the plot, to move the cursor; the readouts at top left show that column's Raman shift and its real wavelength, and the energy diagram follows
  • "X axis": Raman shift is how much energy was lost compared with the laser (cm⁻¹, waves per centimeter); wavelength is the light's real wavelength (nm); switch lasers and see which one stays the same
  • "Measure": the CCD is exposed (fast-forwarded on screen), then the whole spectrum is read out from left to right; measure again with another laser or exposure and the previous one stays for comparison
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。元件的位置、大小與光路長度都是示意(真的光譜儀焦距約 20 到 80 cm,CCD 晶片約 2.7 × 0.7 cm,這裡放大);規格以常見的共軛焦拉曼顯微鏡與原廠的濾光片、CCD 資料為例:雷射 532 與 785 nm,樣品處的功率這裡兩顆都用 10 mW(示意);二向分光鏡在 45° 反射雷射、讓波長較長的光穿過;0° 的邊緣濾光片在雷射波長的 OD 大於 6(只剩不到百萬分之一),離雷射約 90 到 120 cm⁻¹(785 nm 約 60 到 80 cm⁻¹)以內量不到;CCD 致冷到約 −60 °C,一欄像素約 2 cm⁻¹。換雷射時,雷射線濾光片、二向分光鏡、邊緣濾光片與光柵都要換成配那顆雷射的。拉曼光子在畫面上放大了很多倍才看得到。聚苯乙烯的峰位與相對強度取自標準譜,矽約 520.5 cm⁻¹,纖維素與木質素的峰位取自文獻;香草醛只有 588、1511、1592、1665 cm⁻¹ 的峰位有出處,其他小峰與所有樣品的峰寬是示意,香草醛與木粉的峰高、螢光背景的大小也是示意。532 nm 下木質素 1600 cm⁻¹ 的峰另外加強(前共振,倍數是示意)。計數依散射光頻率(數光子時是三次方)與 CCD 在各波長的效率算出,所以每支峰換雷射後變弱的倍數不同。很多機型把樣品區關在罩子裡,門一開雷射就停(Class 1);開放式或接光纖探頭時沒有這層保護。785 nm 看不見,看不見不代表不危險。光譜是模擬的,不是實測數據。

Schematic. Positions, sizes and path lengths are illustrative (a real spectrograph has a focal length of about 20 to 80 cm, and the CCD chip, about 2.7 × 0.7 cm, is enlarged here); specifications follow a common confocal Raman microscope and manufacturers' filter and CCD data: 532 and 785 nm lasers, both set to 10 mW at the sample here (illustrative); a dichroic mirror at 45° reflects the laser and passes longer wavelengths; a 0° edge filter has OD above 6 at the laser line (under a millionth left), and nothing within about 90 to 120 cm⁻¹ of the laser (about 60 to 80 cm⁻¹ at 785 nm) can be measured; the CCD is cooled to about −60 °C, and each pixel column covers about 2 cm⁻¹. Changing lasers also means changing the clean-up filter, dichroic mirror, edge filter and grating to the ones made for that laser. Raman photons are shown far more often than they really occur so you can see them. Polystyrene peak positions and relative intensities come from a standard spectrum, silicon is about 520.5 cm⁻¹, and cellulose and lignin positions come from the literature; for vanillin only the 588, 1511, 1592 and 1665 cm⁻¹ positions are sourced, its other small peaks and all peak widths are illustrative, and so are the vanillin and wood peak heights and the size of the fluorescence background. Under 532 nm the lignin peak at 1600 cm⁻¹ gets an extra boost (pre-resonance, illustrative factor). Counts follow the scattered light's frequency (cubed when counting photons) and the CCD's efficiency at each wavelength, so each peak weakens by a different factor when the laser changes. Many models keep the sample in an enclosure and stop the laser when the door opens (Class 1); open setups and fiber probes do not have that protection. 785 nm is invisible, which does not make it harmless. The spectra are simulated, not measured data.
