AAS 原子吸收光譜儀 每種元素一盞燈

原子吸收量的是溶液裡某一種金屬元素有多少(可量六十多種元素),例如木材防腐劑裡的銅和鉻。溶液被吸進約 2300 °C 的火焰,大部分拆成自由的原子;燈發出只有這種元素會吸收的特定波長(所以每種元素一盞燈),原子越多,光被吸收越多。選好溶液按「測量」:先吸空白歸零,再量標準液做檢量線,最後量未知樣品。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 選溶液與元素:按控制面板,或直接點樣品架上的管子、燈座上的燈
  • 背景校正:關掉時,未知樣品裡的微粒與沒拆開的分子擋掉的光也算成吸收,讀值偏高;鋅 213.86 nm 這種短波長比銅明顯
  • 「測量」:毛細管伸進選好的溶液,吸光度爬到平台後讀 3 秒,再回到空白;標準液的點加到檢量線,未知樣品對回檢量線算出濃度;換溶液或條件再量一次,上一次會留著比較
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Pick the solution and element in the panel, or click a tube in the rack or a lamp on the turret
  • Background correction: when it is off, light blocked by particles and unbroken molecules in the unknown also counts as absorption, so the reading is high; this matters more at short wavelengths such as zinc 213.86 nm than for copper
  • "Measure": the capillary dips into the chosen solution, the absorbance climbs to a plateau and is read for 3 s, then the capillary goes back to the blank; a standard adds a point to the calibration curve, and the unknown is read off the curve; measure again with another solution or setting and the previous trace stays for comparison
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。以常見的單光束火焰式原子吸收光譜儀為例(很多機型是雙光束;燈座一次可裝 4 到 8 盞燈,也有多元素燈);元件的位置、光路、截光器的速度(真的轉得快很多)與原子的大小都是示意。吸光度依廠商方法手冊(1996)列的特徵濃度推算;訊號爬升的快慢、雜訊、火焰自己的光與背景的大小是示意,鋅、鐵、鉻燈的其他譜線位置也是示意。未知樣品的濃度是推算的示意值:CCA 保留量 6.4 kg/m³、木材密度假設 500 kg/m³、0.100 g 木粉消化後定容到 100 mL;鋅是 ACZA 處理材的消化液稀釋 10 倍,鐵的水樣是示意。燃燒器頭長 10 cm(笑氣火焰用 5 cm 的頭)。安全:乙炔火焰不能離開人;真的儀器有火焰偵測與連鎖保護,沒火就自動關氣,火焰上方要有排氣;燈發紫外光,不要直視。

Schematic. Modeled on a common single-beam flame atomic absorption spectrometer (many models are double beam; a turret holds 4 to 8 lamps, and multi-element lamps also exist); part positions, beam paths, the chopper speed (the real one spins much faster) and the size of the atoms are illustrative. Absorbances are calculated from characteristic concentrations listed in a manufacturer's methods manual (1996); how fast the signal rises, the noise, the flame's own light, the background sizes and the other lines of the zinc, iron and chromium lamps are illustrative. The unknowns are estimated, illustrative values: CCA retention 6.4 kg/m³, an assumed wood density of 500 kg/m³, and 0.100 g of wood meal digested and made up to 100 mL; the zinc sample is a digest of ACZA-treated wood diluted 10 times, and the iron water sample is illustrative. The burner head is 10 cm long (a nitrous oxide flame uses a 5 cm head). Safety: never leave an acetylene flame unattended; real instruments have flame sensors and interlocks that shut off the gas if the flame goes out, and need an exhaust above the flame; the lamps give off ultraviolet light, so do not look into them.
