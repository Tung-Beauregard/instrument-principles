FTIR 紅外光譜儀 干涉變光譜

紅外光讓化學鍵振動,每種鍵吸收自己的波數(每公分有幾個波),所以紅外光譜像官能基的指紋。FTIR 不是一個波數一個波數量:整束紅外光走進干涉儀,一面鏡子來回移動,偵測器記下光的強弱起伏(干涉圖),電腦再用傅立葉轉換算回光譜。拖曳活動鏡或按「掃描」看看。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 左右拖曳活動鏡,或用光程差滑桿,看偵測器的訊號怎麼起伏;光程差 0 的地方最亮
  • 「掃描」:活動鏡從一端走到另一端,記下整張干涉圖,再做傅立葉轉換算出光譜;換解析度或平均次數再掃一次,上一條會留著比較
  • 「顯示」:吸光度是樣品除以背景(空的)之後的結果;單光束是兩者各自的光譜,看得到空氣裡的水氣與二氧化碳
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Drag the moving mirror left or right, or use the path difference slider, to see the detector signal rise and fall; it is brightest at zero path difference
  • "Scan": the mirror travels from one end to the other, the whole interferogram is recorded, and a Fourier transform gives the spectrum; scan again with another resolution or number of scans and the previous curve stays for comparison
  • "Show": absorbance is the sample divided by the empty background; single beam shows each of them, including water vapor and carbon dioxide in the air
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

Spectra are simulated from literature band positions, and the Fourier transform between interferogram and spectrum is really computed on this page; it is not measured data.
