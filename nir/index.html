NIR 近紅外光譜儀 從寬峰估成分

近紅外光(約 780 到 2500 nm)比紅光再長一點。化學鍵在中紅外吸收很強;到了近紅外,只剩頻率接近兩倍(略少一點)的泛音,和兩種振動相加的合頻(組合頻),弱幾十到幾千倍,光才鑽得進木粉約 0.1 到 1 mm,所以不用溶解,裝杯就能量。代價是峰又寬又重疊,要先用已知含水率的樣品建立校正模型。拖曳光柵、移動滑桿,或按「掃描」。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 拖曳凹面光柵,或用波長滑桿,改變照到樣品的波長;近紅外光看不見,這裡用橘色畫
  • 含水率滑桿改變樣品裡的水(乾基:以乾重為分母);「顯示」可以換成二次微分(峰變成向下的谷)或校正模型(估計值對參考值)
  • 「掃描」:先量白色參考板(當 100 %),再讓光柵從 1100 擺到 2500 nm;真的一趟約 0.5 秒、通常平均 32 次,這裡第一趟放慢成約 5 秒,其餘快轉;換含水率再掃一次,上一次會留著比較
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Drag the concave grating, or use the wavelength slider, to change the wavelength on the sample; near infrared is invisible, so it is drawn orange
  • The moisture slider changes the water in the sample (dry basis: per dry weight); "Show" switches to the second derivative (peaks become dips) or the calibration model (predicted vs reference)
  • "Scan": the white reference is measured first (as 100%), then the grating swings from 1100 to 2500 nm; a real sweep takes about 0.5 s and usually 32 are averaged, so here the first sweep is slowed to about 5 s and the rest are fast-forwarded; change the moisture and scan again, and the previous curve stays for comparison
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。以常見的桌上型掃描式光柵近紅外光譜儀為例:鹵素燈、凹面全像光柵、分級濾光片(1100 到 1700、1700 到 2500 nm 各一片)、樣品杯坐在石英窗上、光從下方垂直照上去,4 個硫化鉛與 4 個矽偵測器擺在 45°(矽偵測器量可見光,這頁只畫 1100 到 2500 nm)。元件的位置與大小都簡化過,參考板的機構與杯子轉動是示意。峰的位置取自木材的近紅外文獻;峰高、寬度、含水率造成的變化與校正模型都是示意,不是實測數據。

Schematic. Based on a common benchtop scanning-grating NIR spectrometer: halogen lamp, concave holographic grating, order-sorting filters (one for 1100 to 1700 nm, one for 1700 to 2500 nm), a sample cup sitting on a quartz window lit straight from below, and 4 lead sulfide plus 4 silicon detectors at 45° (the silicon ones cover visible light; this page shows only 1100 to 2500 nm). Positions and sizes are simplified; the reference mechanism and the cup rotation are illustrative. Band positions are from the near-infrared literature on wood; heights, widths, the moisture effect and the calibration model are illustrative, not measured data.
