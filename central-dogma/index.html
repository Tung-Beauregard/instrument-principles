中心法則 DNA 複製、轉錄、轉譯

DNA 靠鹼基配對複製自己;要用到某個基因時,先轉錄成 mRNA,再由核糖體照著三個一組的密碼子接出蛋白質。可以自己調世代數看 Meselson 與 Stahl 的離心結果,也可以改一個鹼基,看蛋白質會變成怎樣。

章節

    重點整理

    • DNA 是兩股反向平行的雙股螺旋:骨架是糖與磷酸,鹼基在中間配對,A 與 T 之間兩個氫鍵、G 與 C 之間三個;直徑約 2 nm,每轉一圈約 10 個鹼基對(3.4 nm)。
    • 複製:解旋酶打開雙股,引子酶做一小段 RNA 引子(約 5 到 10 個核苷酸),DNA 聚合酶只能把核苷酸接在 3′ 端;前導股連續合成,延遲股做成一段段岡崎片段,引子換成 DNA 後由接合酶接起來。大腸桿菌每秒約接 1000 個核苷酸。
    • Meselson 與 Stahl(1958)讓大腸桿菌在 ¹⁵N 裡長 14 代後換到 ¹⁴N,用氯化銫密度梯度離心:第 1 代只有中間密度的一條帶,第 2 代中間與輕各一半,符合半保留複製。
    • 轉錄:RNA 聚合酶從啟動子開始讀模板股,不需要引子;RNA 由 5′ 往 3′ 長,用 U 不用 T。真核生物的前驅 mRNA 還要加 5′ 帽、約 200 個 A 的尾巴,並剪掉內含子。
    • 轉譯:核糖體一次讀一個密碼子,tRNA 的反密碼子和它配對並帶來胺基酸。64 個密碼子裡 61 個對應胺基酸、3 個是終止(UAA、UAG、UGA);AUG 是甲硫胺酸,也是起始。改一個鹼基常見三種結果:同義、錯義、無義突變。
    • DNA is a double helix of two antiparallel strands: the backbone is sugar and phosphate, and the bases pair in the middle, with two hydrogen bonds between A and T and three between G and C. It is about 2 nm across, with about 10 base pairs per turn (3.4 nm).
    • Replication: helicase opens the double strand, primase makes a short RNA primer (about 5 to 10 nucleotides), and DNA polymerase can only add nucleotides to the 3′ end. The leading strand is made continuously; the lagging strand is made in pieces, the Okazaki fragments, and once the primers are replaced with DNA, ligase joins them. E. coli adds about 1,000 nucleotides per second.
    • Meselson and Stahl (1958) grew E. coli on ¹⁵N for 14 generations, moved it to ¹⁴N and spun the DNA in a cesium chloride density gradient: generation 1 had a single band of intermediate density, and generation 2 was half intermediate and half light, as semiconservative replication predicts.
    • Transcription: RNA polymerase starts at the promoter and reads the template strand, with no primer needed; RNA grows from 5′ to 3′ and uses U instead of T. In eukaryotes the pre-mRNA also gets a 5′ cap and a tail of about 200 A's, and its introns are cut out.
    • Translation: the ribosome reads one codon at a time, and a tRNA whose anticodon pairs with it brings the amino acid. Of the 64 codons, 61 code for amino acids and 3 are stops (UAA, UAG, UGA); AUG codes for methionine and is also the start. Changing one base commonly has one of three results: a silent, missense or nonsense mutation.
