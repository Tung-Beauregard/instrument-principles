EPR 電子順磁共振儀 找出不成對的電子

大部分分子的電子兩兩成對,磁性互相抵消;自由基和某些金屬離子有落單(不成對)的電子,像一根小磁鐵。放進磁場,它只能順著或逆著磁場,兩種狀態的能量差跟著磁場變大。微波頻率固定、慢慢加大磁場,能量差剛好等於一份微波的能量時,電子吸收微波翻面。和 NMR 是同一個道理,只是看電子,不是原子核。拖曳電磁鐵或磁場滑桿試試。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 改磁場:用磁場滑桿,或在 3D 裡左右拖曳電磁鐵(細調),或在譜圖上點一下;1 mT = 10 G(高斯)
  • 「掃描」:微波頻率固定,磁場約 6 秒由低掃到高,把每個磁場的訊號連成譜;換調變幅度或樣品再掃一次,上一條會留著比較
  • 「調變幅度」:磁場小抖動的大小。比線寬小很多(一成以下)時線形最真,但訊號弱、雜訊明顯;和線寬差不多時訊號最好;太大(過調變)線會被拉寬、變形,靠得近的線會黏在一起
  • 「顯示」:一次微分是儀器直接畫出來的;吸收是把它積分回去。電子多少要比吸收曲線下的面積(積分兩次),不能比高度
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Change the field with the slider, by dragging the electromagnet left or right in 3D (fine steps), or by clicking the spectrum; 1 mT = 10 G (gauss)
  • "Scan": with the microwave frequency fixed, the field sweeps from low to high in about 6 s and the signal at every field joins into a spectrum; scan again with another modulation or sample and the previous curve stays for comparison
  • "Modulation": the size of the field's small wiggle. Much smaller than the linewidth (under a tenth) gives the truest shape but a weak, noisy signal; about the linewidth gives the best signal; too large (overmodulation) widens and distorts the line, and close lines merge
  • "Show": the first derivative is what the instrument draws; absorption is that curve integrated back. To compare amounts of electrons, compare the area under the absorption (a double integral), not the height
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。數字取自幾台代表性的 X 頻帶(約 9.5 GHz)連續波 EPR 儀與文獻;磁鐵、共振腔與微波橋的大小和位置是示意,霍爾探針的位置是假設的。微波看不見:往共振腔的畫成藍綠色,反射回來的畫成橘色(頻率相同,只是方便分辨),反射的強弱放大畫。箭頭是電子的小磁鐵;室溫下順著磁場的其實只比逆著的多約萬分之八,畫面誇大了。g 值與分裂取自文獻:DPPH 固體 g 2.0036;TEMPOL 水溶液 g 2.0056、氮的分裂 1.66 mT;DMPO-OH 四條線 1:2:2:1、間距 1.49 mT(g 值沒有查到可靠的數字,中心位置是示意);木質素粉末 g 2.0040 到 2.0043、線寬約 0.7 mT。TEMPOL 與 DMPO-OH 的線寬、各樣品的訊號大小(假設電子一樣多)與雜訊是示意。水會大量吸收微波(像微波爐加熱食物),所以水溶液放在扁平樣品槽裡。

Schematic. Numbers come from several representative X-band (about 9.5 GHz) continuous-wave EPR instruments and the literature; the sizes and positions of the magnet, cavity and bridge are illustrative, and the Hall probe's position is assumed. Microwaves are invisible: those going to the cavity are drawn blue-green and the reflected ones orange (same frequency, colored only to tell them apart), and the reflection is exaggerated. The arrows are the electrons' tiny magnets; at room temperature only about 8 in 10,000 more point with the field than against it, so the picture exaggerates. g values and splittings are from the literature: solid DPPH g 2.0036; TEMPOL in water g 2.0056 with a nitrogen splitting of 1.66 mT; DMPO-OH four lines 1:2:2:1, 1.49 mT apart (no reliable g value was found, so its center position is illustrative); lignin powder g 2.0040 to 2.0043, linewidth about 0.7 mT. The linewidths of TEMPOL and DMPO-OH, the signal size of each sample (assuming equal numbers of electrons) and the noise are illustrative. Water absorbs microwaves strongly (as in a microwave oven), so water solutions go in a flat cell.
