木材三大成分 纖維素、半纖維素與木質素

木材乾重九成以上是三種大分子:纖維素、半纖維素和木質素。從細胞壁一層一層的構造看起,接著看纖維素怎麼排成微纖絲、半纖維素用的糖差在哪一號碳、木質素的單體怎麼接成網狀,以及 β-O-4 這種用號碼命名的鍵;最後看三者怎麼連在一起、實驗室怎麼把它們分開來量。

    重點整理

    • 木材乾重九成以上是三種大分子:纖維素(約 40% 到 50%)、半纖維素(約 20% 到 30%)、木質素(針葉樹約 26% 到 32%,闊葉樹約 20% 到 28%);其餘是萃取物(林產化學也叫抽出物,溫帶樹種多半在 10% 以下)和不到 1% 的灰分。
    • 細胞壁由外到內是中膠層、初生壁、次生壁外層(S1)、中層(S2)、內層(S3)。S2 最厚,微纖絲和細胞長軸的夾角(微纖絲角)小,約 5° 到 30°,木材順紋的強度主要靠它。木質素在中膠層與細胞隅角濃度最高,但大約七成的木質素在次生壁。
    • 纖維素是葡萄糖用 β(1→4) 接成的直鏈,聚合度約一萬,一條鏈長約 5 μm。很多條鏈靠氫鍵排成約 3 nm 寬的微纖絲,大部分結晶,所以纖維素不溶於水、很強;它也是地球上最多的有機物。
    • 半纖維素用好幾種糖,用碳的編號就能認:甘露糖和葡萄糖只差 2 號碳、半乳糖只差 4 號碳、木糖沒有 6 號碳、阿拉伯糖是 5 個碳的五員環、4-O-甲基葡萄糖醛酸的 6 號碳是羧基。闊葉樹以木聚糖為主,針葉樹以半乳葡甘露聚糖為主;鏈短(約 100 到 200 個糖)、有支鏈和乙醯基、不結晶。
    • 木質素由三種單體(對香豆醇、松柏醇、芥子醇)經自由基偶合長成不規則的立體網狀,單元分別叫 H、G、S,只差在 3、5 號碳有沒有甲氧基。木質素習慣從接側鏈的碳編 1 號,側鏈是 α、β、γ;命名法則的苯酚則從接 OH 的碳編 1 號。
    • 木質素單元之間的鍵用位置命名:最多的是 β-O-4,A 單元的 β 碳經過一個氧接到 B 單元的 4 號碳。闊葉樹的 S 單元 5 號碳有甲氧基,碳和碳的鍵少、β-O-4 多,比較容易拆開。
    • 半纖維素靠氫鍵貼在微纖絲上,又和木質素以醚鍵、酯鍵、糖苷鍵相連(木質素-碳水化合物複合體),三者連成一體,所以木材耐用、不容易分解。
    • 含量是照方法定出來的:溶劑萃取得萃取物;72% 硫酸水解後剩下的固體是酸不溶木質素(Klason 木質素);亞氯酸鈉去掉木質素得全纖維素,再用 17.5% 氫氧化鈉溶液分出 α-纖維素。
    • More than 90% of the dry weight of wood is three large molecules: cellulose (about 40% to 50%), hemicellulose (about 20% to 30%) and lignin (about 26% to 32% in softwoods, about 20% to 28% in hardwoods). The rest is extractives (mostly under 10% in temperate species) and less than 1% ash.
    • From the outside in, the cell wall has the middle lamella, the primary wall, and the outer (S1), middle (S2) and inner (S3) layers of the secondary wall. S2 is the thickest, and the angle between its microfibrils and the long axis of the cell (the microfibril angle) is small, about 5° to 30°; the strength of wood along the grain comes mainly from this layer. Lignin is most concentrated in the middle lamella and the cell corners, but about 70% of the lignin is in the secondary wall.
    • Cellulose is a straight chain of glucose joined β(1→4), with a degree of polymerization of about 10,000; one chain is about 5 μm long. Many chains line up through hydrogen bonds into microfibrils about 3 nm wide that are mostly crystalline, so cellulose does not dissolve in water and is very strong; it is also the most abundant organic substance on Earth.
    • Hemicellulose uses several sugars, and carbon numbers tell them apart: mannose differs from glucose only at carbon 2, galactose only at carbon 4, xylose has no carbon 6, arabinose is a five-membered ring with 5 carbons, and carbon 6 of 4-O-methylglucuronic acid is a carboxyl group. Hardwoods mainly have xylan and softwoods mainly galactoglucomannan; the chains are short (about 100 to 200 sugars), carry side chains and acetyl groups, and are not crystalline.
    • Lignin grows from three monomers (p-coumaryl alcohol, coniferyl alcohol and sinapyl alcohol) by radical coupling into an irregular three-dimensional network. Their units are called H, G and S, and differ only in whether carbons 3 and 5 carry a methoxy group. Lignin is usually numbered from the carbon that holds the side chain (carbon 1), with the side-chain carbons called α, β and γ; in systematic (IUPAC) naming, a phenol is numbered from the carbon that holds the OH.
    • Bonds between lignin units are named by position: the most common is β-O-4, in which the β carbon of unit A joins carbon 4 of unit B through an oxygen. In hardwoods, the S units have a methoxy group on carbon 5, so there are fewer carbon–carbon bonds and more β-O-4, and the lignin is easier to break apart.
    • Hemicellulose clings to the microfibrils through hydrogen bonds and is joined to lignin by ether, ester and glycosidic bonds (lignin–carbohydrate complexes). The three are linked into one whole, which is why wood lasts and is hard to break down.
    • The amounts are defined by the method: solvent extraction gives the extractives; the solid left after hydrolysis in 72% sulfuric acid is acid-insoluble lignin (Klason lignin); removing the lignin with sodium chlorite gives holocellulose, and a 17.5% sodium hydroxide solution then separates out α-cellulose.
