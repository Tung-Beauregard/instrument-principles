二維 NMR 誰和誰相連

一維 NMR 看得出分子裡有幾種氫,但峰常擠在一起,也看不出哪個氫接在哪個碳上。二維 NMR 用同一台儀器,在兩個脈衝之間多一段一次比一次長的等待 t1,收很多次訊號,再做兩次傅立葉轉換,畫成一張地圖;對角線以外的交叉峰,就是彼此相連的兩個原子。選一種實驗按「測量」,或拖曳第二個脈衝改 t1。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 左右拖曳脈衝序列上 t1 之後的脈衝,或用 t1 滑桿,看 FID 的大小怎麼隨 t1 起伏
  • 滑鼠停在(或點一下)二維圖的峰上,看是哪兩個原子,結構式會標出來;HMBC 本身分不出隔 2 或 3 個鍵,鍵數是照結構數的
  • 「測量」:t1 一次比一次長,收齊每一條 FID,再沿 t2、t1 各做一次傅立葉轉換,堆疊攤平成二維圖;換實驗或增量次數再測一次,上一次會留著比較
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Drag a pulse after t1 on the pulse sequence left or right, or use the t1 slider, to see the FID grow and shrink with t1
  • Hover over (or tap) a peak on the 2D map to see which two atoms it links; they light up on the structure. HMBC itself cannot tell 2 bonds from 3; the bond counts come from the structure
  • "Measure": t1 grows a little each time, every FID is recorded, then one Fourier transform along t2 and one along t1 flatten the stack into a 2D map; measure again with another experiment or number of increments and the previous map stays for comparison
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。和 NMR 頁同一類 500 MHz 儀器(以 Bruker AVANCE III 500 搭配 5 mm 探頭為例):11.7 T、¹H 500.13 MHz、¹³C 125.76 MHz;磁鐵縮小、探頭與樣品管放大畫。二維譜依文獻化學位移模擬(香草醛溶在 CDCl₃;¹H 同 NMR 頁,¹³C 取自猶他大學 NMR 中心);交叉峰的有無依猶他大學(CDCl₃)與 BMRB(DMSO-d₆)的實測圖,強弱是示意;琥珀色的上一次只在縱軸是同一種原子時畫。t1 畫得比實際長很多(實際最長只有約 1 到 100 ms),沿 t1、t2 起伏的快慢也是示意。實驗時間是估算:每次等待 2 秒,COSY 每個增量收 1 條 FID、HSQC 收 2 條各 2 次、HMBC 收 1 條 4 次,不含鎖場、勻場等準備。探頭裡還有產生梯度磁場的線圈,用來挑出要的訊號。

Schematic. The same kind of 500 MHz instrument as on the NMR page (an example is a Bruker AVANCE III 500 with a 5 mm probe): 11.7 T, ¹H at 500.13 MHz, ¹³C at 125.76 MHz; the magnet is drawn smaller and the probe and sample tube larger. The 2D spectra are simulated from literature chemical shifts (vanillin in CDCl₃; ¹H as on the NMR page, ¹³C from the University of Utah NMR center); which cross peaks exist follows measured spectra from Utah (CDCl₃) and BMRB (DMSO-d₆), and their strengths are illustrative; the amber previous map is drawn only when the vertical axis is the same kind of atom. t1 is drawn far longer than it is (at most about 1 to 100 ms), and how fast things swing along t1 and t2 is illustrative. Run times are estimates: a 2 s wait each time, COSY records 1 FID per increment, HSQC 2 FIDs of 2 scans, HMBC 1 FID of 4 scans, not counting locking and shimming. The probe also has a coil that makes field gradients, used to pick out the wanted signal.
