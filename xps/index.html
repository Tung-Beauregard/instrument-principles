XPS X 射線光電子能譜儀 表面幾奈米的化學

X 射線把樣品原子內層的電子打出來(光電效應)。量電子飛出來的能量(動能),就能推回它原本被綁得多緊(束縛能):每種元素不同,接了氧的碳又比接碳的高一點。電子在固體裡跑不遠,只有最表面幾奈米的出得來,所以 XPS 只看表面;整台在超高真空裡,表面才不會被氣體弄髒。拖曳束縛能滑桿或點譜圖,按「掃描」量整張譜。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 拖曳束縛能滑桿,或在譜圖上點一下,改變分析器放過去的電子;譜圖的橫軸照慣例左邊大、右邊小
  • 「範圍」:全譜從 1100 到 0 eV,看有哪些元素;窄譜只看碳 1s(金看 4f),看化學位移。「電荷中和」關掉時,不導電的樣品帶正電,峰會偏移、變寬
  • 「掃描」:分析器一個能量一個能量數電子,約 6 秒畫出整張譜(真的要幾分鐘);換樣品或中和槍再掃一次,上一次會留著比較
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Drag the binding energy slider, or click the spectrum, to choose which electrons the analyzer lets through; by convention the spectrum's axis runs from high on the left to low on the right
  • "Range": a survey runs from 1100 to 0 eV to show which elements are there; a narrow scan looks only at carbon 1s (gold 4f) to see the chemical shift. With the "Neutralizer" off, a sample that does not conduct charges up, and its peaks shift and widen
  • "Scan": the analyzer counts electrons one energy at a time and draws the whole spectrum in about 6 seconds (a real one takes minutes); scan again with another sample or neutralizer setting and the previous spectrum stays for comparison
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。元件以常見的實驗室 XPS 為例(單色化的鋁 Kα 光源、彎曲的石英晶體單色器、180° 半球形分析器、微通道板偵測器,例如 Thermo K-Alpha、Kratos AXIS Supra);位置、大小與分析器的半徑都是示意。真空約 10⁻⁷ 到 10⁻⁸ Pa;X 射線照到的點幾十到幾百微米;全譜的通過能量 200 eV、窄譜 50 eV。鋁 Kα 1486.6 eV;金 4f7/2 的 83.96 eV 是 ISO 15472 的校正值;不導電的樣品把 C–C 定在 285.0 eV(也有人用 284.8 eV)。濾紙的碳分峰依纖維素的結構計算,另加 5% 的 C–C 當表面汙染(示意);樺木與 200 °C 真空熱處理 4 小時的樺木,O/C 與分峰比例取自文獻(Yang 等 2018,用鎂 Kα 量的),峰的位置與寬度是示意。O/C 用靈敏度因子 2.46(NPL)由面積換算,是用模型的峰面積算的。動能用儀器的功函數 4.5 eV 計算(示意);中和槍關掉時偏移 24 eV 也是示意,實際可從零點幾到數百 eV。有些機型的中和槍同時送出低能的氬離子。離子槍用來清潔金屬表面或做縱深分析;單顆的氬離子會把有機物打壞,木材這類樣品要用氬團簇。計數率與雜訊都是示意。

Schematic. The parts follow a common laboratory XPS (monochromated aluminum Kα source, bent quartz crystal monochromator, 180° hemispherical analyzer, microchannel plate detector; for example Thermo K-Alpha, Kratos AXIS Supra); positions, sizes and the analyzer radius are illustrative. Vacuum about 10⁻⁷ to 10⁻⁸ Pa; the X-ray spot is tens to hundreds of micrometers; pass energy 200 eV for surveys and 50 eV for narrow scans. Al Kα 1486.6 eV; gold 4f7/2 at 83.96 eV is the ISO 15472 calibration value; for samples that do not conduct, C–C is set to 285.0 eV (some labs use 284.8 eV). The filter paper's carbon components are calculated from the structure of cellulose, plus 5% C–C as surface contamination (illustrative); for birch and birch heated in vacuum at 200 °C for 4 hours, O/C and the component shares come from the literature (Yang et al. 2018, measured with magnesium Kα), and peak positions and widths are illustrative. O/C is converted from areas with a sensitivity factor of 2.46 (NPL), using the model's peak areas. Kinetic energy uses an instrument work function of 4.5 eV (illustrative); the 24 eV shift with the neutralizer off is also illustrative, real shifts range from tenths of an eV to hundreds of eV. Some neutralizers also send low-energy argon ions. The ion gun cleans metal surfaces or profiles depth; single argon ions damage organic matter, so samples like wood need argon clusters. Count rates and noise are illustrative.
