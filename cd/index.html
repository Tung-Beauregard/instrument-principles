CD 圓二色光譜儀 分辨左右手

有些分子和自己的鏡像疊不起來,就像左手和右手,稱為手性分子。一對純的鏡像分子,熔點、沸點、紅外光譜都一樣,碰到偏光或酵素這類手性的東西才不同。旋光儀量偏振光的振動方向被轉了幾度;圓二色光譜儀量左旋、右旋圓偏振光被吸收的量差多少(圓二色性,化學名詞是圓偏光二色性)。切換模式、拖曳檢偏鏡,或按「掃描」。

  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Switch between the CD and Rotation modes; in CD the slider sets the wavelength, in Rotation the analyzer slider turns the analyzer, or drag the analyzer in 3D left or right

示意圖。元件以常見的桌上型圓二色光譜儀為例(規格參考 JASCO J-1500 與 Applied Photophysics Chirascan):150 W 級氙燈、晶體石英雙稜鏡單色器兼作起偏、熔融石英光彈調變器約 50 kHz、光電倍增管與鎖相放大器,光路通氮氣(氧會吸收遠紫外光,燈的紫外光也會把氧變成傷鏡面的臭氧)。專用的旋光儀傳統用鈉燈的 589 nm 黃光,這裡把兩種量法放在同一台上方便比較。樟腦磺酸以 0.06% 樟腦磺酸銨、1 cm 光徑為例(290.5 nm 約 +190 mdeg,由文獻的 Δε 算出),(−) 型由鏡像對稱推得;肌紅蛋白只有 222 nm 的強度有文獻值,其餘峰形、β 褶板整條、峰寬、吸光度、HT 與雜訊都是示意。旋光度由文獻的比旋光度算出(α-蒎烯 20 °C 純液體、蔗糖 10 g/100 mL),讀數每 180° 重複一次,實務上換管長再確認。比旋光度的完整單位是 deg mL g⁻¹ dm⁻¹;要和濃度無關的 CD 單位時用 [θ] = 3298 × Δε。偏振的畫法、光的波長與調變的速度都放慢放大過。

Schematic. The parts follow a common benchtop CD spectrometer (specifications from the JASCO J-1500 and Applied Photophysics Chirascan): a 150 W class xenon lamp, a double crystal quartz prism monochromator that also polarizes, a fused quartz photoelastic modulator at about 50 kHz, a photomultiplier and a lock-in amplifier, with a nitrogen purge (oxygen absorbs far UV, and the lamp's UV turns oxygen into ozone that damages mirrors). A dedicated polarimeter traditionally uses the 589 nm yellow light of a sodium lamp; both methods are shown on one instrument for comparison. Camphorsulfonic acid is 0.06% ammonium camphorsulfonate in a 1 cm cell (about +190 mdeg at 290.5 nm, calculated from literature Δε); the (−) form is its mirror image. For myoglobin only the 222 nm strength is from the literature; the other band shapes, the whole β-sheet curve, band widths, absorbance, HT and noise are illustrative. Rotations are calculated from literature specific rotations (α-pinene at 20 °C, neat; sucrose 10 g/100 mL); the reading repeats every 180°, so in practice a second tube length is used to confirm. The full unit of specific rotation is deg mL g⁻¹ dm⁻¹; for a concentration-free CD unit, [θ] = 3298 × Δε. The polarization drawings, the wavelength of light and the modulation are slowed down and enlarged.
