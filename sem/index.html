SEM 掃描式電子顯微鏡 一點一點掃出影像

光學顯微鏡受限於光的波長,放大約一千倍就到頂;SEM 改用電子。電磁透鏡把電子束聚成很小的一點,掃描線圈讓它在樣品上一行一行掃過,每一點打出的二次電子越多,畫面上那一點就越亮。電子也會打出 X 射線,量它的能量(EDS)就知道那一小塊有哪些元素。按「掃描」拍一張,再點影像做 EDS。

操作
  • 滑鼠:拖曳旋轉視角;右鍵或按住 Shift 拖曳平移;滾輪縮放
  • 觸控板:雙指滑動平移、兩指捏合縮放;觸控螢幕:單指旋轉、雙指平移與縮放
  • 鍵盤:方向鍵或 W A S D 移動、Q E 轉向、+ − 縮放,按住 Shift 加快;R 回到預設視角
  • 換樣品或放大倍率:影像先變成預覽(快速掃描,雜訊多);換偵測器或電壓:上一張影像留著,按「掃描」再拍
  • 點影像上任一處做 EDS:電子束停在那一點,約 3 秒累積出能譜;影像上的虛線圈是 X 射線大約來自的範圍
  • 「掃描」:電子束由上往下一行一行掃過,約 5 秒拍好一張,新影像會一行一行蓋過舊的;換個點或換電壓再做 EDS,上一次會留著比較
  • 「導覽」:自動播一遍,內容和影片相同;按 Esc 結束
  • Mouse: drag to rotate the view; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom
  • Trackpad: swipe with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom; touch screen: one finger rotates, two fingers pan and zoom
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D to move, Q E to turn, + − to zoom, hold Shift to go faster; R resets the view
  • Change the sample or magnification and the image becomes a live preview (fast scan, noisy); change the detector or voltage and the last image stays until you scan again
  • Click anywhere on the image for EDS: the beam stops at that point and builds a spectrum in about 3 s; the dashed circle shows roughly where the X-rays come from
  • "Scan": the beam sweeps line by line from top to bottom and takes an image in about 5 s, drawing over the old one; try EDS at another point or voltage and the previous spectrum stays for comparison
  • "Tour": plays through automatically, the same as the video; press Esc to end it

示意圖。以常見的鎢絲燈絲 SEM(加速電壓 0.3 到 30 kV)與矽漂移式 EDS 偵測器為例;元件的位置、大小、工作距離與電子束的粗細都放大或簡化過。木材影像是程式依文獻尺寸畫的,不是照片:針葉樹材早材管胞約 41 µm、壁約 3.2 µm,晚材約 15.5 µm、壁約 7 µm,有緣紋孔約 14 µm,櫸木的早材導管約 300 到 400 µm;結晶 8 到 15 µm 是依細胞大小推估的,常見是草酸鈣。防腐木以 CCA 處理的柳杉邊材為例(銅約 1.5%、鉻約 0.8%、砷約 1.8%,推算值)。放大倍率以 128 mm 寬的照片計;同一張影像放在 358 mm 寬的螢幕上,倍率要寫成約 2.8 倍。能譜的峰位置是查表值,峰高、雜訊都是示意;5 秒拍一張、3 秒一個能譜是為了教學縮短的,實際拍一張約 50 到 100 秒,能譜通常要幾十秒。電子鑽進去的深度是用 Kanaya-Okayama 公式算的約略值。場發射槍用強電場把電子從很尖的針尖拉出來,冷場發射在室溫工作,電子束更細、更亮,但要超高真空。

Schematic. Based on a common tungsten-filament SEM (0.3 to 30 kV) with a silicon drift EDS detector; positions, sizes, working distance and beam width are enlarged or simplified. The wood images are drawn by the program from literature sizes, not photographs: softwood earlywood tracheids about 41 µm with 3.2 µm walls, latewood about 15.5 µm with 7 µm walls, bordered pits about 14 µm, Zelkova earlywood vessels about 300 to 400 µm; crystals of 8 to 15 µm are estimated from cell size and are usually calcium oxalate. The treated wood is CCA-treated Japanese cedar sapwood (about 1.5% copper, 0.8% chromium, 1.8% arsenic, estimated). Magnification refers to a 128 mm wide photo; the same image on a 358 mm wide screen would be labeled about 2.8 times higher. Peak positions are tabulated values; peak heights and noise are illustrative. The 5 s image and 3 s spectrum are shortened for teaching: a real photo takes about 50 to 100 s and a spectrum usually tens of seconds. Electron depths are rough values from the Kanaya-Okayama formula. A field-emission gun pulls electrons from a very sharp tip with a strong electric field; a cold field emitter works at room temperature and gives a finer, brighter beam but needs ultra-high vacuum.
