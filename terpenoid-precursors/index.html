萜類前驅物 MEP 與 MVA 兩條路

萜類的 5 碳積木 IPP 與 DMAPP 在植物細胞裡有兩個來源:質體的 MEP 路徑從丙酮酸與 G3P 開始,細胞質的 MVA 路徑從乙醯輔酶 A 開始。這一頁算兩條路的碳與能量帳,看接長鏈時兩種積木怎麼配,怎麼用抑制劑與同位素標記判斷萜類的碳從哪條路來,最後是 P450 怎麼把雙萜骨架氧化成針葉樹的樹脂酸。

章節

    重點整理

    先看:萜類合成酶(它的第 2 章講 IPP 與 DMAPP 怎麼接成 GPP、FPP、GGPP;這一頁講這兩塊積木從哪裡來、之後還有哪些修飾)

    See first: Terpene synthases (its chapter 2 shows how IPP and DMAPP join into GPP, FPP and GGPP; this page covers where these two building blocks come from and what modifications follow)

    • 兩條路:MVA 路徑在細胞質(HMGR 在內質網膜上,最後幾步與 IDI 在過氧化體),主要供應倍半萜、三萜與固醇;MEP 路徑在質體,主要供應異戊二烯、單萜、雙萜(含吉貝素)、類胡蘿蔔素與葉綠素的側鏈。被子植物的葉綠素側鏈、類胡蘿蔔素與三萜幾乎只來自各自的路,其他萜類常常兩條路都有份。
    • MVA:3 乙醯輔酶 A + 2 NADPH + 3 ATP → IPP + CO₂,HMGR 常是限速步驟,也是史他汀類的標的。MEP:丙酮酸 + G3P + NADPH + CTP + ATP + 4 個還原態鐵氧化還原蛋白 → IPP 或 DMAPP + CO₂,DXR 是 fosmidomycin 的標的,最後的 HDR 做出 IPP:DMAPP 約 85:15(菸草細胞)。
    • 5n 個碳的鏈要 1 個 DMAPP 加 n − 1 個 IPP;原料裡 DMAPP 的比例 d₀ 不夠時,IDI 要把 1/n − d₀ 的原料從 IPP 轉成 DMAPP(只算單一產物的帳)。
    • 判斷碳的來源:抑制劑只是線索,擋住一邊時另一邊也會變;同位素標記較直接。每個 5 碳單位帶標記的機率為 p 時,n 個單位的產物裡剛好 k 個帶標記的比例是 C(n,k)·pk·(1 − p)n−k。金魚草的倍半萜出現 +6,作者據此認為質體送出的是 IPP 或 DMAPP,不只是 GPP。
    • 樹脂酸:質體裡的雙萜合成酶做出 abietadiene 等骨架,內質網上的 CYP720B 把 18 號碳氧化三次(醇、醛、酸)。火炬松的 CYP720B1 只做後兩步,西加雲杉的 CYP720B4 三步都做;雲杉莖外層的雙萜類 92% 是樹脂酸。
    • 碳帳:從三碳化合物算起,MEP 留下 5/6 的碳,MVA 只留 5/9;把做 ATP、NADPH 用掉的糖也算進去,理論質量產率 MEP 約 30%、MVA 約 25%。
    • Two pathways: the MVA pathway is in the cytoplasm (HMGR sits in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane; the last steps and IDI are in peroxisomes) and mainly supplies sesquiterpenes, triterpenes and sterols; the MEP pathway is in the plastid and mainly supplies isoprene, monoterpenes, diterpenes (including gibberellins), carotenoids and the side chain of chlorophyll. In flowering plants, the chlorophyll side chain, carotenoids and triterpenes come almost only from their own pathway; other terpenoids often draw on both.
    • MVA: 3 acetyl-CoA + 2 NADPH + 3 ATP → IPP + CO₂; HMGR is often the rate-limiting step and is the target of statins. MEP: pyruvate + G3P + NADPH + CTP + ATP + 4 reduced ferredoxins → IPP or DMAPP + CO₂; DXR is the target of fosmidomycin, and the last enzyme, HDR, makes IPP:DMAPP at about 85:15 (tobacco cells).
    • A chain of 5n carbons needs 1 DMAPP plus n − 1 IPP; when the DMAPP fraction d₀ in the supply falls short, IDI has to convert a fraction 1/n − d₀ of the supply from IPP to DMAPP (counting a single product only).
    • Tracing the carbon: inhibitors are only clues, since blocking one pathway also changes the other; isotope labeling is more direct. If each 5-carbon unit is labeled with probability p, the fraction of an n-unit product with exactly k labeled units is C(n,k)·pk·(1 − p)n−k. Snapdragon sesquiterpenes showed +6, so the authors concluded that the plastid exports IPP or DMAPP, not only GPP.
    • Resin acids: a diterpene synthase in the plastid makes skeletons such as abietadiene, and a CYP720B on the endoplasmic reticulum oxidizes carbon 18 three times (alcohol, aldehyde, acid). Loblolly pine CYP720B1 does only the last two steps, while Sitka spruce CYP720B4 does all three; 92% of the diterpenoids in the outer stem of the spruce are resin acids.
    • Carbon budget: counting from three-carbon compounds, MEP keeps 5/6 of the carbon and MVA only 5/9; including the sugar spent on ATP and NADPH, the theoretical mass yield is about 30% for MEP and 25% for MVA.
