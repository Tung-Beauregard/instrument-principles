在植物裡驗證基因功能 大腸桿菌做得出來,不等於植物裡這樣用

純化的酵素加上受質,測到的是「它能做什麼」。植物細胞還有胞器位置、前驅物供應與其他酵素的搭配,所以功能要回到植物裡確認:農桿菌把基因送進本氏菸草葉片暫時表現、GC-MS 比對空載體看新的峰、GFP 看酵素在哪一區,再用病毒誘導基因沉默把它關掉看產物掉多少。

章節

    重點整理

    先看:基因選殖與蛋白表現(在大腸桿菌裡做出酵素)、萜類合成酶、CRISPR、PCR 與電泳(qPCR)。

    Read first: Gene cloning and protein expression (making the enzyme in E. coli), Terpene synthases, CRISPR, PCR and gel electrophoresis (qPCR).

    • 植物細胞把萜類前驅物分在兩區:質體的 MEP 途徑供應 GPP(C10)與 GGPP(C20),細胞質的 MVA 途徑供應 FPP(C15)。酵素在哪一區就只遇得到那一區的受質,所以試管裡的產物未必是植物體內的產物。金魚草兩支序列 95% 相同的酵素,細胞質那支做 nerolidol、質體那支做 linalool。
    • 單萜與雙萜合成酶前端有一段質體轉運胜肽(冷杉的三支約 60 到 70 個胺基酸,絲胺酸占 11 到 22%、酸性殘基只有 2 到 4 個),進質體後被切掉;高度保守的連續兩個精胺酸大約就是成熟蛋白的起點。倍半萜合成酶沒有這一段,留在細胞質。
    • 農桿菌的 T-DNA 兩端是 25 個鹼基對的邊界序列,邊界之間的東西會被送進植物細胞。雙元載體把 T-DNA 放在另一個可以複製的質體上,vir 基因留在卸掉致病性的 Ti 質體;傷口汁液的酸性(pH 5.0 到 5.8)與單環酚類(例如乙醯丁香酮)經 VirA 與 VirG 打開 vir 基因。T-DNA 不嵌進染色體也會表現幾天,這就是暫時表現。
    • 浸潤的葉片會啟動轉錄後基因沉默:DCL4 與 DCL2 把轉殖基因的雙股 RNA 切成 21 到 22 個核苷酸的 siRNA,裝進 AGO 後切掉 mRNA,螢光從第 4 天起一路下降。番茄叢矮病毒的 p19 以二聚體夾住這些 siRNA 雙股,一起表現時第 4、7、10 天的螢光都高約一個數量級。
    • 看產物一定要有只打空載體的對照葉片:銀杏與蓖麻的雙萜合成酶在菸草表現時,空載體的葉片在同一位置測不到產物。前驅物不夠時一起打上游的 DXS 與 GGPPS,雲杉的雙萜產量增加 3 倍、菸草的 cembratrien-ol 增加 3.5 倍。
    • 反過來把基因關掉:TRV 的兩條 RNA 分工,TRV2 上接一段 200 到 1000 bp 的目標片段,農桿菌打進 3 週大的葉片,2 到 3 週後新長出來的葉子被沉默。沉默八氫番茄紅素去飽和酶(PDS)會擋掉類胡蘿蔔素、讓葉綠素被光氧化,葉子漂白,是這一批到底有沒有效的目視對照。
    • 樹木的世代長:歐洲雲杉要 20 到 25 年、赤松 8 到 20 年才進入生殖期,楊樹約 6 年、橡約 60 年。楊樹是模式樹(基因體 480 到 550 Mb、插枝可繁殖、轉殖容易,第一個被定序的樹);松杉類體胚發生的效率低,馬尾松至今無法轉殖,功能改在本氏菸草裡驗。
    • Plant cells keep terpenoid precursors in two compartments: the MEP pathway in the plastid supplies GPP (C10) and GGPP (C20), the MVA pathway in the cytoplasm supplies FPP (C15). An enzyme only meets the substrates of the compartment it sits in, so the product in a test tube need not be the product in the plant. Two snapdragon enzymes whose sequences are 95% identical make nerolidol in the cytoplasm and linalool in the plastid.
    • Monoterpene and diterpene synthases begin with a plastid transit peptide (about 60 to 70 amino acids in three fir enzymes, 11% to 22% serine and only 2 to 4 acidic residues), which is cut off once the protein is inside the plastid; a highly conserved pair of arginines marks roughly where the mature protein begins. Sesquiterpene synthases have no such segment and stay in the cytoplasm.
    • The T-DNA of Agrobacterium is flanked by 25 bp border sequences, and whatever lies between the borders is delivered into the plant cell. A binary vector carries the T-DNA on a second plasmid that can replicate on its own, while the vir genes stay on a disarmed Ti plasmid; acidic wound sap (pH 5.0 to 5.8) and simple phenolics such as acetosyringone switch the vir genes on through VirA and VirG. The T-DNA is expressed for a few days without integrating into a chromosome: this is transient expression.
    • An infiltrated leaf starts post-transcriptional gene silencing: DCL4 and DCL2 cut the double-stranded RNA of the transgene into siRNA of 21 to 22 nucleotides, which load into AGO and cut the matching mRNA, so the fluorescence falls from day 4 onward. The p19 protein of tomato bushy stunt virus clamps these siRNA duplexes as a dimer; expressed along with the transgene, it keeps the fluorescence about ten times higher on days 4, 7 and 10.
    • Reading a product always needs a control leaf infiltrated with the empty vector: when the diterpene synthases of ginkgo and castor bean are expressed in tobacco, nothing is detected at the same position in empty-vector leaves. When precursors run short, adding the upstream DXS and GGPPS raised the diterpene yield 3-fold in spruce and cembratrien-ol 3.5-fold in tobacco.
    • The other direction is switching a gene off: the two RNAs of TRV divide the work, TRV2 carries a 200 to 1,000 bp piece of the target gene, Agrobacterium goes into 3-week-old leaves, and the leaves that grow 2 to 3 weeks later are silenced. Silencing phytoene desaturase (PDS) blocks carotenoids and lets light destroy the chlorophyll, so the leaves bleach: the visual check that the batch worked.
    • Trees have long generations: Norway spruce takes 20 to 25 years and Scots pine 8 to 20 years to become reproductive, poplar about 6 years and oak about 60. Poplar is the model tree (genome 480 to 550 Mb, propagated from cuttings, easy to transform, the first tree sequenced); somatic embryogenesis works poorly in conifers, Masson pine still cannot be transformed, and its genes are tested in N. benthamiana instead.
