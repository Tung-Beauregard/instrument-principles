PCR 與電泳 把一小段 DNA 複製幾十億份

PCR 用三個溫度反覆循環,把兩條引子之間的那一段 DNA 一輪一輪加倍;做完用洋菜膠電泳看產物多長,或用 qPCR 邊做邊量,推回樣品裡原本有多少。

自己調整

章節

    重點整理

    • PCR 的一個循環:變性(94 到 98 °C,雙股分開)、黏合(引子黏到模板,溫度約比引子的熔解溫度低 5 °C)、延伸(聚合酶照模板接上核苷酸,Taq 聚合酶最適 70 到 80 °C,常用 72 °C)。
    • 每個循環理論上把數量加倍,通常做 25 到 35 輪;後段原料用完,數量不再加倍(平原期)。
    • 洋菜膠電泳:DNA 帶負電,往正極移動,短的片段跑得遠;和已知長度的分子量標準比對就知道產物長度。
    • qPCR 每輪量一次螢光,起始量越多越早超過門檻(Ct 越小);效率 100% 時,起始量差 10 倍,Ct 差約 3.32 輪。
    • 進階:引子的 Tm 有兩種估法。Wallace 規則 2(A+T) + 4(G+C) 只看組成;最近鄰法(SantaLucia 1998)把相鄰兩對鹼基的 ΔH°、ΔS° 加起來,再依鹽與引子濃度算出 Tm,同一條引子在 50 mM 與 1 M Na⁺ 下差將近 15 °C。預測只供設計參考,黏合溫度要用實驗決定;引子的 3′ 端不要和自己或另一條引子互補,否則會形成引子二聚體。
    • 進階:相對定量的 2−ΔΔCt 法(Livak 與 Schmittgen 2001)假設目標基因與參考基因的效率都接近 100%;效率不同時改用 Pfaffl 2001 以實測效率當底數的比值。用 SYBR Green 時要看熔解曲線(−dF/dT):一個窄而尖的峰表示產物單純,70 多 °C 的矮寬峰多半是引子二聚體。
    • One PCR cycle: denaturation (94 to 98 °C; the two strands separate), annealing (primers bind the template, at about 5 °C below the primers' melting temperature) and extension (the polymerase adds nucleotides along the template; Taq polymerase works best at 70 to 80 °C, usually 72 °C).
    • In theory each cycle doubles the amount, and a run usually has 25 to 35 cycles; late in the run the ingredients are used up and the amount stops doubling (the plateau).
    • Agarose gel electrophoresis: DNA is negatively charged and moves toward the positive electrode, and short pieces travel farther; comparing with a size marker of known lengths gives the length of the product.
    • qPCR measures fluorescence every cycle. The more starting material, the sooner the signal crosses the threshold (the smaller the Ct); at 100% efficiency, a 10-fold difference in starting amount shifts Ct by about 3.32 cycles.
    • Advanced: a primer's Tm can be estimated two ways. The Wallace rule, 2(A+T) + 4(G+C), only looks at composition; the nearest-neighbor method (SantaLucia 1998) adds up ΔH° and ΔS° for each pair of neighboring base pairs and then calculates Tm from the salt and primer concentrations, so the same primer differs by nearly 15 °C between 50 mM and 1 M Na⁺. Predictions only guide the design; the annealing temperature is settled by experiment. A primer's 3′ end should not be complementary to itself or to the other primer, or primer dimers form.
    • Advanced: the 2−ΔΔCt method of relative quantification (Livak and Schmittgen 2001) assumes the target and reference genes both have efficiencies close to 100%; when they differ, the Pfaffl 2001 ratio uses the measured efficiencies as the base instead. With SYBR Green, check the melting curve (−dF/dT): one narrow, sharp peak means a clean product, while a low, broad peak in the 70s °C is usually primer dimer.
