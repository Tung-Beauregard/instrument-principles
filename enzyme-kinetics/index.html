酵素動力學 Km 與 kcat 怎麼量

一支酵素有多快、對受質多敏感,要從「初速度對受質濃度」的曲線求出來。這份教材以萜類合成酶的活性測定為例,從反應的模型、取樣與定量,講到怎麼擬合出 Km 與 kcat、怎麼判斷結果可不可信,以及抑制劑與突變怎麼改變這些參數。

章節

    重點整理

    先看:萜類合成酶、基因選殖與蛋白表現

    See first: Terpene synthases, Gene cloning and protein expression

    • 模型是 E + S ⇌ ES → E + P。動力學量的是初速度 v0:產物對時間曲線開頭直線段的斜率,這時受質還沒用掉多少、產物抑制與酵素失活也還沒有影響。實作上先做時間曲線,把反應時間定在直線段裡。
    • 萜類合成酶的測法:緩衝液裡放酵素、GPP 與 Mg2+,上面蓋一層含內標的戊烷收集揮發的產物,搖勻後取戊烷層打進 GC-MS,用產物峰對內標峰的面積比換算產物量。
    • v0 = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S]);Km 是速度到 Vmax 一半時的受質濃度。Km = (k−1 + kcat) / k1,只有 kcat 遠小於 k−1 時才約等於 ES 的解離常數。
    • kcat = Vmax / [E]t,是每個酵素分子每秒轉換幾個受質;kcat/Km 是受質很少時的效率。萜類合成酶很慢:這裡列的四支 kcat 約每秒 0.02 到 0.5 次,文獻中酵素的中位數約每秒 10 次。
    • 不要用雙倒數圖擬合:取倒數會把低濃度點的誤差放大(約 δ/v02)。直接用非線性迴歸擬合原始數據,而且濃度要跨過 Km 的兩側;點都在 Km 以下時,Km 與 Vmax 分別都估不準。
    • 抑制劑:α = 1 + [I]/Ki。競爭型 Km 變大 α 倍、Vmax 不變;非競爭型 Vmax 變小 α 倍、Km 不變;反競爭型兩者都變小 α 倍。雙倒數圖適合拿來看型態。
    • 西加雲杉的 PsTPS-3car2 把口袋裡 596 號的 Leu 換成 Phe,主產物從 (+)-3-carene 變成 (−)-sabinene,kcat 增加約 6.6 倍,Km 幾乎不變。
    • The model is E + S ⇌ ES → E + P. Kinetics measures the initial rate v0: the slope of the straight early part of the product-versus-time curve, while little substrate has been used and product inhibition and enzyme inactivation have not yet set in. In practice, run a time course first and keep the reaction time within the straight part.
    • Assaying a terpene synthase: the enzyme, GPP and Mg2+ go into buffer under a layer of pentane that holds an internal standard and collects the volatile products; after mixing, the pentane layer is injected into the GC-MS, and the area ratio of the product peak to the internal standard peak converts to the amount of product.
    • v0 = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S]); Km is the substrate concentration at which the rate reaches half of Vmax. Km = (k−1 + kcat) / k1, which is close to the dissociation constant of ES only when kcat is much smaller than k−1.
    • kcat = Vmax / [E]t is how many substrate molecules each enzyme molecule converts per second; kcat/Km is the efficiency when substrate is scarce. Terpene synthases are slow: the four listed here have kcat values of about 0.02 to 0.5 per second, while the median for enzymes in the literature is about 10 per second.
    • Do not fit with the double-reciprocal plot: taking reciprocals magnifies the errors of the low-concentration points (to about δ/v02). Fit the raw data directly by nonlinear regression, with concentrations on both sides of Km; when all the points are below Km, neither Km nor Vmax can be estimated well on its own.
    • Inhibitors: α = 1 + [I]/Ki. Competitive: Km grows α-fold and Vmax is unchanged; noncompetitive: Vmax shrinks α-fold and Km is unchanged; uncompetitive: both shrink α-fold. The double-reciprocal plot is good for seeing the type.
    • In Sitka spruce PsTPS-3car2, replacing Leu at position 596 in the pocket with Phe changes the main product from (+)-3-carene to (−)-sabinene, raises kcat about 6.6-fold and leaves Km almost unchanged.
